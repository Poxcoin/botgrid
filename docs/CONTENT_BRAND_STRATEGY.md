# KADO — Content & Brand Strategy

**Core principle:** every piece of content is a product, not an ad.

Each video, post, thread SOLVES a problem (educates, transparently shows work, demonstrates infrastructure) — AND happens to position the brand. Distribution comes from utility.

---

## Why this matters more than ads

| Reality | Implication |
|---|---|
| Crypto users have ad-blindness immune system | Performance marketing CAC = $$$ |
| Founders/investors look for SUBSTANCE | Cheap ads = cheap signal |
| Twitter/TikTok rewards educational depth | Viral comes from teaching, not selling |
| First-mover content lives forever on Google | One great video = passive lead gen for years |

**Math:** 1 viral educational video (50K+ views) = ~$50K equivalent paid acquisition cost. Built once, distributes forever.

## Brand positioning (anti-hype)

### Voice
- **Technical-honest** (not corporate cautious)
- **Quietly confident** (we built defensible infrastructure, no need to shout)
- **Numbers > adjectives** ("$2,225 → $0 drift" not "we improved drift")
- **Anti-cherry-pick** ("96 trades, 39.6% WR" not just "70% WR news bot")
- **Ukrainian/founder vulnerability OK** (no fake American startup polish)

### What we are
- "Banking-grade infrastructure для retail trading"
- "Audit-defensible billing platform"
- "Quant-fund methodology, retail price point"

### What we are NOT
- Bot store (3Commas-style)
- Signal seller (paid Telegram channels)
- "Get rich with AI" (BitConnect-style)
- Pump-and-dump signaling

### One-liner (use everywhere)
**EN:** "The first retail crypto trading platform with banking-grade billing infrastructure."
**UA:** "Перша retail-платформа з audit-grade обліком прибутків."
**RU:** "Первая retail-платформа с банковским уровнем учёта."

## Visual brand system

### Colors (locked)
```
background:  #050505  (rich black, NOT pure black — softer)
foreground:  #e8e8e8  (warm white, easier than #fff)
accent:      #00b894  (KADO green — single accent, never 2+)
warning:     #f87171  (only when showing losses or alerts)
muted:       #666     (subtitles, metadata)
```

### Typography
```
hero:    Inter Display Bold  80-120pt  letter-spacing -0.04em
heading: Inter Bold          32-48pt
meta:    JetBrains Mono      14-18pt   UPPERCASE  letter-spacing 0.12em
body:    Inter Regular       18-24pt   line-height 1.5
code:    JetBrains Mono      16pt      line-height 1.4
```

### Layout rules
- **Margins ≥ 60px** every direction
- **Single focal point** per frame
- **One accent color** per video (always green, never mixed)
- **Negative space ≥ 50%** of frame
- **Logo "KADO" only** — text mark, no graphic logo yet

### Motion
- Fades only (0.2-0.4s in, 0.15-0.2s out)
- NO swooshes, zooms, shakes, particle effects
- Text appears static OR scrolls slowly (max 50px/sec)
- Cuts > transitions

### Sound
- **Tier 1 (preferred):** Silence with room tone (ambient hum)
- **Tier 2:** Single ambient pad (Drone Boy, Brian Eno-style)
- **Tier 3:** Minimal beat (4-5 BPM lower than typical TikTok)
- **NEVER:** "Aesthetic" lo-fi loops, viral sound clips, voice memes

### Workflow (per video, 20-40 min)
1. Write script (3 min)
2. Record screen / voice (10 min)
3. Import to CapCut, add text overlays per template (10-15 min)
4. Export 1080×1920 (9:16) for TikTok, or 1080×1080 (1:1) for Twitter, or 1920×1080 for LinkedIn
5. Post with caption (5 min)

## Content pillars (themes to rotate)

### Pillar 1: Infrastructure deep-dives (40%)
Topics: event-sourced billing, drift detection, Bayesian quality monitor
Hook: technical curiosity
Example: "How we made our DB match Bybit to the cent"

### Pillar 2: Anti-marketing transparency (30%)
Topics: bot kills, failed experiments, honest PnL
Hook: refreshing honesty in spammy space
Example: "We killed 4 of our own trading bots in 24 hours"

### Pillar 3: Methodology / education (20%)
Topics: Bayesian sourcing, regret minimization, walk-forward backtest
Hook: teaching > selling
Example: "What is a Regret Engine and why no retail platform has one"

### Pillar 4: Building in public (10%)
Topics: solo founder daily, recruiting cofounder, fundraise journey
Hook: relatability + investor inbound
Example: "Day 3 of 90 toward YC track record"

## Anti-patterns (NEVER do)

1. ❌ "Crypto millionaire" / lambo / yacht footage
2. ❌ "10x your portfolio" / "secret algorithm" / "guaranteed profits"
3. ❌ Pump-and-dump signals (publicly OR through "exclusive" channels)
4. ❌ Stock images / generic B-roll
5. ❌ Multiple accent colors in one video
6. ❌ Yellow circles + arrows (TikTok crypto-bro aesthetic)
7. ❌ Voice modulation effects (echo, reverb, robot voice)
8. ❌ Engagement bait ("comment YES if you want part 2")
9. ❌ Buying followers / inflating metrics
10. ❌ Cross-posting WITHOUT format adaptation (vertical TikTok ≠ horizontal LinkedIn)

## Founder-cam content (later, optional)

If you want to add face-cam later:
- Single static angle (no jump cuts)
- Soft natural lighting (window > ring light)
- Neutral background (clean wall OR blurred bookshelf)
- 1080p phone vertical = sufficient
- Always pair face with screen recording B-roll (cut between)
- Hook in first 1.5s — your face on screen for ~3 seconds max before cutting to product

But: face-cam optional. Many viral founders (Pieter Levels, DHH historically) succeed without face-cam. Brand voice consistency > parasocial trust.
