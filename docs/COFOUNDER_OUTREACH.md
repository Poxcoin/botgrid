# Cofounder Outreach Playbook — ML/AI Co-Founder Hunt

**Goal:** Hire ML/AI Co-Founder for KADO (5-15% equity, fully remote).
**Timeline:** 30-60 days from start of outreach.
**Method:** Personalized LinkedIn DMs + cold emails. NO mass blast.

---

## Strategy

**Don't apply for cofounder. Recruit one.**

Top ML researchers and quants don't browse careers pages. They get **introduced** or **DM'd with specific, personalized pitch**. Volume matters less than targeting + quality of opening message.

**Math:** 50 well-targeted DMs → 10-15 responses → 3-5 calls → 1 closed cofounder = realistic 60-day target.

## Outreach Calendar (30-day campaign)

**Week 1: Sourcing**
- Build target list of 100 prospects across 7 personas
- Verify each: LinkedIn URL + Twitter (if any) + 1 specific hook
- Tools: LinkedIn Sales Navigator trial (free 30d), Apollo.io free tier, manual search

**Week 2: First 50 DMs**
- 10 per day × 5 days
- Personalize EACH (no copy-paste — paste-then-edit)
- Track: send time, response, status

**Week 3: Follow-up + 50 more**
- DMs that didn't reply 7d → 1 follow-up ("just want to confirm you saw this")
- Add 50 new prospects this week
- Calendar bookings start

**Week 4: Calls + closing**
- 5-10 calls scheduled
- Each call: 30 min, end with mutual decision
- Goal: 1-2 second-round meetings booked

## Response Handling

**Scenario: "Tell me more"**
→ Reply with: 1) /track-record link, 2) PITCH_DECK.md PDF, 3) suggest 20-min call with 2 time slots.

**Scenario: "Why crypto?"**
→ "Non-custodial SaaS = lower regulatory burden vs traditional asset management. Plus retail crypto users are price-insensitive on performance fees (vs stock traders who fight over basis points)."

**Scenario: "Why should I leave [current job]?"**
→ "Equity 5-15% on a $5M cap at pre-seed = $250K-$750K paper value at next round. More importantly: you get to build the ML methodology layer from scratch with full ownership."

**Scenario: "Show me your team"**
→ "Solo founder right now — that's why I'm recruiting you. We've built the infrastructure foundation (event-sourced billing, /track-record live). I need someone to scale the ML side. You'd be Co-Founder, not employee."

**Scenario: "I have a job offer at [other startup]"**
→ "Understood. Quick question: would you give me 20 min to make my case before you decide? I'll respect your timeline."

**Scenario: "Not interested"**
→ "Thanks for the quick reply. If KADO doesn't fit, would you know anyone in your network who might?" (referral ask)

## Red Flags (skip these prospects)

- Active 2y+ at YC startup raising Seed → already taken
- Multiple cofounder ad-hoc gigs in past 12 months → cofounder-hopper
- Public posts complaining about all past employers → toxic
- ML background but never shipped production → academic only
- Asks about salary in first reply → not founder material

---

## Quality Bar (must-haves)

- 2+ years ML/AI hands-on (paper OR production OR OSS)
- Comfortable with 12-18 months pre-revenue
- Built or shipped SOMETHING (not just research)
- Crypto-curious (don't need expert, but no anti-crypto stance)
- Available 30+ hrs/week minimum
- Lives in compatible timezone (Europe ±4h, OR full async OK)
